2. Who we share it with

Only service providers that run the site for us: [[HOSTING_PROVIDER]] (website hosting), Aiven (database, messaging and search hosting), and [[OTHER_PROCESSORS]]. They process data on our instructions. We disclose data to authorities only when the law requires it.

3. Your consent

We ask for consent before collecting anything that is not needed to run the service. Boxes are never pre-ticked. You can withdraw consent at any time through the Privacy choices link in the footer. Withdrawing is as easy as giving consent: one click, no account needed.

4. Your rights

You may ask to access a summary of your data, correct it, erase it, withdraw consent, raise a grievance, and nominate another person to exercise these rights for you if you cannot. Write to our grievance officer. We respond within [[RESPONSE_DAYS]] days (never more than 90). If you are not satisfied you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

5. Children

StockSure is not meant for anyone under 18. If you are under 18, please use it only with a parent or guardian, who must give consent for us to process your data. If we learn we hold a child's data without verified parental consent we will delete it.

6. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS (TLS). Staff keys are never stored in the browser. We limit who can access data and keep a breach response plan.

7. If there is a data breach

If personal data is breached we will tell each affected person without delay: what happened, the likely consequences, what we have done, what you can do, and who to contact. We will also report it to the Data Protection Board of India.

8. Changes

We will post changes here and update the date above. For material changes we will ask for consent again where required.
