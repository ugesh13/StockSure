How to complain

  1. Open the Help chat at the bottom right and describe the problem. The assistant will try to fix it or log your complaint.
  2. If you are not satisfied, choose Talk to a person in the chat. It passes your conversation to our team so you need not repeat yourself.
  3. You can always write to the grievance officer directly using the details above.

Our timelines

We acknowledge your complaint within 48 hours and resolve it within one month. For privacy and data requests we respond within [[RESPONSE_DAYS]] days and never later than 90 days.

Data requests

To access, correct or erase your data, withdraw consent, or nominate someone to act for you, write to the grievance officer with your customer ID or the contact you used. If we do not resolve it you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
